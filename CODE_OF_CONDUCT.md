# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
for everyone who contributes code, hardware designs, documentation, data, or discussion.

## In short
- Be respectful. Harassment, discrimination, sexualised language or imagery, threats, and doxxing are not accepted.
- Participants under 18 take part only through a teacher- or staff-run programme ([SAFETY_MINORS.md](SAFETY_MINORS.md)).
  Do not post personal information, photos, or contact details of minors in issues, pull requests, or data.
- Never publish anyone's personal data (see [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)).

## Reporting
Report a concern by email to the maintainers (사단법인 이타서울): **CONTACT_EMAIL**.
Reports are read by the maintainers only and handled confidentially. We aim to reply within 7 days.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or data that break this code, and may
block a participant temporarily or permanently. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant
Enforcement Guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
